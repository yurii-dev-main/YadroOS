## Employee Management

### Viewing the Employee Directory
**Navigate to**: Sidebar → HR → Employees

Browse all team members with filters for department, role, and status. Switch between card and table layouts to suit your workflow.

[Screenshot: Employee directory]

**Key actions**:
- Search by name, email, or employee ID
- Filter by department, manager, location, or employment type
- Export to CSV for HR reporting

### Adding New Employees
1. Click "Add Employee"
2. Fill required fields (name, email, role)
3. Assign manager and department
4. Upload onboarding documents (offer letter, ID)
5. Click "Create Employee"

The system automatically invites the employee to set their password and profile.

### Managing Employee Profiles
Open any employee card to view detailed tabs:
- **Overview**: Personal info, contact data, employment status
- **Documents**: Contracts, NDAs, certificates
- **Activity**: Logged training, time entries, performance notes
- **Compensation**: Salary history, bonus records

Use inline editing for quick updates or click "Edit" for full form edits.

### Offboarding
When an employee leaves:
1. Open their profile → Actions → "Start Offboarding"
2. Select offboarding checklist items (knowledge transfer, equipment return)
3. Set final working day and reason
4. Deactivate account (access revoked immediately or scheduled)
5. Archive records after completion

## Training & Development

### Managing Training Catalog
**Navigate to**: HR → Training → Catalog

- Create courses with descriptions, modules, and attachments
- Assign trainers and set availability (mandatory/optional)
- Tag courses by department, skill, or compliance requirement

### Enrolling Employees
1. From a course page, click "Assign"
2. Choose employees or entire teams
3. Set due dates and reminders
4. Send enrollment notifications

Employees receive email and in-app reminders. Progress is tracked automatically when lessons are completed or quizzes submitted.

### Tracking Progress
- Dashboard shows completion rates, overdue courses, and certification expirations
- Filter by department or manager to identify gaps
- Export completion reports for compliance audits

## Time Tracking

### Recording Time
**Navigate to**: HR → Time → Timesheets

Employees can:
- Log hours manually per project or task
- Use timers for real-time tracking
- Submit weekly timesheets for approval

Managers review submissions, request changes, and approve within the same interface.

### Leave Management
- Employees submit PTO, sick leave, and remote work requests
- Approvers receive notifications and can approve/decline with comments
- Calendar view highlights team availability and upcoming absences

### Integrations
Sync time data with payroll and project management tools via configured integrations (e.g., accounting module, Jira).

## Performance Reviews

### Creating Review Cycles
1. Go to HR → Performance → Cycles
2. Click "New Cycle"
3. Define cycle name, duration, and participants
4. Choose review template (self-review, manager review, peer feedback)
5. Launch cycle and notify participants

### Conducting Reviews
- Employees complete self-assessments
- Managers provide evaluations and set goals
- Optional peer reviewers submit structured feedback
- Attach supporting documents (KPI dashboards, project retrospectives)

### Calibration & Sign-Off
Use the calibration dashboard to compare ratings across teams, ensure fairness, and finalize outcomes. Sign off digitally and share results with employees.

### Goal Tracking
Create SMART goals, assign owners, and monitor progress over time. Link goals to performance cycles for seamless evaluation.

While payroll calculations occur in the Accounting module, HR contributes:

Review payroll drafts before finalization to confirm accuracy.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Keep employee records up to date to avoid compliance issues
- Schedule regular training refreshers for mandatory certifications
- Encourage timely timesheet submissions with automated reminders
- Document performance feedback consistently for each employee
- Collaborate with Accounting to reconcile payroll and benefits data

## Troubleshooting

### Employee cannot log in
- Confirm the account is active
- Reset password from profile actions
- Check if SSO provider is reachable

### Training assignments missing
- Verify the employee's department matches the course audience
- Ensure the training cycle is still active
- Re-sync integrations if using LMS connectors

### Timesheet approval stuck
- Check manager delegation settings
- Ensure the timesheet period is open
- Contact support if approval workflow errors persist
